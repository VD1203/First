    Four states between a drifted book and a signed order.

    Scroll to step through them — forward or back. The frame is drawn live on a canvas, so there is no video and no frame to load.

    Positions, in from custodyEvery account in the household is reconciled against the custodian's overnight file. Cost basis, lot dates and pending activity arrive with it.
    Drift, against the mandateEach sleeve is scored against its target and its tolerance band. Two are outside. Five are not, and Meridian leaves them alone.
    Orders, with the lots chosenThe correction is sized, then the lots are picked — highest basis first, harvesting the short-term loss and clearing the wash-sale window.
    Approval, then the fileAn advisor approves, edits or rejects. Approved orders leave as a custodian file and every decision lands in an append-only ledger.

    01The mechanism, in words
    1. ReadPositions, in from custody

      Accounts reconcile against the custodian's overnight file — cost basis, lot dates and pending activity included.

    2. MeasureDrift, against the mandate

      Every sleeve is scored against its target and tolerance band. Sleeves inside the band are left alone.

    3. DraftOrders, with the lots chosen

      The correction is sized, then lots are selected highest-basis-first, harvesting losses and clearing wash-sale windows.

    4. ApproveApproval, then the file

      An advisor approves, edits or rejects. Only approved orders become a custodian file, and every decision is logged.

    03Platform

    Four systems, one approval.

    Meridian is not one model with a chat box in front of it. It is four deterministic systems whose output a language model has to explain — and which an advisor has to sign.
